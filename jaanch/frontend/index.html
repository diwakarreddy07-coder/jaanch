What this tool can’t tell you

It uses only what you enter. It cannot see inside Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp, cannot prove an account is fake or official, and scores are starting heuristics, not probabilities. A Low result means no red flags were found, not that something is safe.

Never share an OTP or password. Unknown links are never opened by this tool. Everything stays in this browser.
